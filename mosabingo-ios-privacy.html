MosaBingo iOS 隱私政策

本政策說明 SLAIAC studio 提供的 MosaBingo iPhone 與 iPad App 如何處理資料。

iOS 版免費下載、無廣告，不需註冊遊戲帳號。離線數字盤、一般連線模式與參加瘋狂模式均可免費使用；瘋狂模式自行開局提供免費試用及一次購買的永久開局權。本政策適用於 iOS／iPadOS 版；Android 版另有隱私政策。

1. 本機遊戲資料

偏好設定、離線數字盤、標記與顏色、App 產生的隨機安裝／玩家識別碼，以及最近的區域遊戲與調盤資料，保存在裝置的 App 專用儲存空間。我們沒有自架伺服器接收或保存這些遊戲資料，也不使用廣告、追蹤或遊戲分析 SDK。App 保存的遊戲資料目錄排除於系統備份，不提供雲端遊戲同步。

2. 附近多人連線與權限

附近多人遊戲透過同一區域網路探索及玩家裝置間的連線運作，支援 iPhone、iPad 與跨平台裝置。App 會請求區域網路存取權。同一場遊戲的裝置交換玩家識別碼、規則、完整數字盤、選號、回合、投票與結果，以同步遊戲及判定勝負；即使其他玩家的盤面未顯示，完整盤面仍會同步給參與裝置。

連線資料使用加密傳輸；附近裝置的身分沒有額外帳號驗證，請與可信任的玩家使用可信任的區域網路。App 不要求 GPS 位置、聯絡人、相機或麥克風權限。區域網路權限只用於連線模式，可在系統設定中管理。

3. Apple 購買服務

試用領取、付費購買及恢復購買由 Apple 的 App Store 購買服務處理。App 使用 Apple 提供的交易與商品資訊確認購買權益。我們不接收付款卡片、Apple 帳號密碼或完整付款資料，也不將 Apple 購買紀錄上傳至自架伺服器。Apple 依其政策處理商店交易、帳號與相關服務資料，請參閱 Apple 隱私政策。

4. 第三方時間服務

為支援限時試用，App 可能直接連線至 TimeAPI（timeapi.io）或 UTCTime（utctime.app）取得目前時間。服務提供者會接收 IP 位址及一般連線資訊，並可能保存請求時間、請求路徑、回應狀態或裝置／用戶端資訊，用於提供服務、維護安全及診斷問題。App 不會向這些服務傳送遊戲數字盤、選號或 Apple 購買紀錄。

服務提供者依其政策處理及保存資料。UTCTime 說明 API 請求會產生一般伺服器存取紀錄，並定期輪替；本 App 不指定其保存天數。請參閱 TimeAPI 隱私政策與 UTCTime 隱私政策，並依其管道提出資料請求。

5. 保存、刪除與您的選擇

本機設定與盤面保留至您修改、重設或刪除 App；離開房間會清除本機保存的該場恢復資料。刪除 App（不是只卸載 App）可移除本機 App 資料。其他參與裝置上的遊戲副本須由各裝置自行清除。刪除 App 不會刪除 Apple 的購買紀錄；交易與帳號資料請透過 Apple 的管理及支援管道處理。

App 不要求姓名、生日、電子郵件或遊戲帳號。若兒童使用購買功能，請由家長或監護人協助，並可使用 Apple 的購買限制。離線數字盤不需要網路；購買、恢復購買及試用確認可能需要網路。

6. 支援資料、聯絡與更新

若有隱私問題，或希望了解、修正或刪除您主動提供的支援資料，請寄信至 SLAIAC.SERVICE@gmail.com。您的電子郵件地址、訊息及主動附上的資料會用於回覆與處理請求；保存期間依處理請求及適用法規所需。請勿傳送密碼或不必要的敏感資料。我們無法從伺服器取回只存在您裝置上的遊戲資料。

MosaBingo iOS Privacy Policy

This policy explains how MosaBingo for iPhone and iPad, provided by SLAIAC studio, handles data.

The iOS app is free to download, has no ads and requires no game account. Offline boards, normal multiplayer and joining Crazy mode are free. Hosting Crazy mode offers a free trial and permanent access through a one-time purchase. This policy covers iOS and iPadOS; Android has a separate privacy policy.

1. Game data on your device

Preferences, offline boards, marks and colors, an app-generated random installation/player identifier, and recent nearby game and board arrangement data are stored in app-specific device storage. We do not operate a server that receives or stores this game data, and do not use advertising, tracking or game analytics SDKs. The app's saved game data directory is excluded from system backups. Cloud game synchronization is not provided.

2. Nearby multiplayer and permissions

Nearby multiplayer uses discovery on the same local network and connections between player devices, including iPhone, iPad and devices on other platforms. The app requests Local Network access. Participants exchange player identifiers, rules, complete boards, number selections, turns, votes and results to synchronize play and determine winners. Complete boards are synchronized even when other players' boards are hidden in the interface.

Game traffic is encrypted. Nearby device identities have no additional account authentication, so play with trusted people on a trusted local network. The app does not request GPS location, contacts, camera or microphone permissions. Local Network access is used only for multiplayer and can be managed in system settings.

3. Apple purchase services

Trial claims, paid purchases and purchase restoration are handled by Apple's App Store purchase services. The app uses transaction and product information supplied by Apple to confirm purchased access. We do not receive payment card details, Apple account passwords or complete payment information, and do not upload Apple purchase records to a server we operate. Apple handles store transactions, account and related service data under its own policies. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-party time services

To support the limited trial, the app may connect directly to TimeAPI (timeapi.io) or UTCTime (utctime.app) to obtain the current time. Providers receive your IP address and ordinary connection information, and may retain request times, requested paths, response status and device/client information to operate the service, maintain security and diagnose issues. The app does not send these providers game boards, number selections or Apple purchase records.

Providers process and retain data under their own policies. UTCTime states that API requests produce ordinary server access logs that are rotated regularly; this app does not specify their retention in days. See the TimeAPI Privacy Policy and UTCTime Privacy Policy, and use their channels for requests about their data.

5. Retention, deletion and your choices

Local settings and boards remain until changed, reset or deleted with the app. Leaving a room clears its locally saved reconnection data. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes local app data. Game copies on other participating devices must be cleared on those devices. Deleting the app does not delete Apple's purchase records; use Apple's account management and support channels for transaction and account data.

The app does not require your name, birth date, email address or a game account. Parents or guardians should assist children with purchase features and can use Apple's purchase restrictions. Offline boards do not require internet access; purchasing, restoring and confirming trial access may require it.

6. Support information, contact and updates

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct or delete support information you voluntarily provide, contact SLAIAC.SERVICE@gmail.com. Your email address, message and any information you choose to attach are used to reply and handle the request, and retained as needed for support and applicable legal requirements. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. We cannot retrieve game data stored only on your device from a server.

Policy changes appear here with an updated date. For help using the app, visit MosaBingo Support.
