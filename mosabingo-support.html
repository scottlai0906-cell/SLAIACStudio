離線數字盤怎麼使用？

建立 1～5 張數字盤，自訂行列大小、順序或亂序數字。點擊數字即可標記或取消，並可切換標記顏色。玩法由你決定，離線數字盤不會自動計分或判定勝負。盤面保存在這台裝置，之後可以繼續使用。

附近多人遊戲找不到其他玩家？

  • 所有玩家使用同一 Wi-Fi 的區域網路，並開啟相同模式；支援 2～5 位玩家及 iPhone、iPad、Android 跨平台對戰。
  • 請更新至最新相容版本，保持 App 在前景，並在系統要求時允許區域網路存取。
  • 若先前拒絕權限，請到系統設定重新允許。訪客 Wi-Fi、路由器的裝置隔離或防火牆可能阻止玩家互相連線，請改用允許裝置互通的可信任網路。

瘋狂模式有哪些免費功能？

離線數字盤、一般模式開局與參加，以及參加瘋狂模式均免費。iOS 版只有自行開局瘋狂模式需要有效開局權，可主動領取 7 天免費試用，或一次購買永久解鎖，不必先試用。試用到期不會自動扣款或續期；價格以購買前顯示的 App Store 當地售價為準。Android 版免費、無內購，以廣告營利。

iOS 如何恢復購買？

使用原本購買的 Apple 帳號，在 MosaBingo 設定或瘋狂模式開局權面板點「恢復購買」。重裝或更換裝置後可重新取得同一帳號的有效權益。恢復購買不會重設試用期限，取消購買或等待核准也不會直接取得永久權限。

顯示「暫時無法確認權限」或開始遊戲反灰？

請確認網際網路與 Apple 購買服務可用，再點「重試」或「恢復購買」。權限檢查失敗只限制 iOS 瘋狂模式自行開局，其他免費功能仍可使用。開始遊戲還需符合至少兩名玩家、玩家已準備及同步完成等條件；即使已取得開局權，仍須符合房間條件。

購買或退款問題？

付款與退款由 Apple 處理，可透過 Apple 回報問題查詢或申請。提交退款申請不代表退款已核准。若問題是 App 未顯示權益，請先恢復購買，再將相關提示與 App 版本寄給我們；不需要提供帳號密碼。

資料與隱私

遊戲盤面與設定保存在裝置；刪除 App 可能移除本機資料，且沒有遊戲雲端備份。請參閱 iOS 隱私政策或 Android 隱私政策。

MosaBingo Support

Need help? Email SLAIAC.SERVICE@gmail.com with your app version, device model, operating system version, steps to reproduce the issue and relevant screenshots. Do not send Apple or Google passwords, payment card details or unnecessary personal information.

How do offline boards work?

Create 1–5 boards with your choice of rows, columns and ordered or shuffled numbers. Tap numbers to mark or unmark them and choose a marking color. You decide the rules: offline boards do not automatically score or determine winners. Boards are saved on this device for later use.

Why can't I find nearby players?

  • Connect everyone to the same local Wi-Fi network and open the same mode. Nearby games support 2–5 players across iPhone, iPad and Android.
  • Update to the latest compatible versions, keep each app in the foreground and allow Local Network access when requested.
  • If you previously denied permission, allow it in system settings. Guest Wi-Fi, router client isolation or a firewall may prevent devices from connecting. Use a trusted network that allows communication between devices.

What is free in Crazy mode?

Offline boards, hosting and joining normal mode, and joining Crazy mode are free. On iOS, only hosting Crazy mode requires valid access. You may claim a free seven-day trial or unlock permanent access with a one-time purchase, without taking the trial first. The trial never automatically charges or renews. The purchase price is the local App Store price shown before purchase. Android is free, has no in-app purchases and is supported by advertising.

How do I restore iOS purchases?

Use the Apple account that originally made the purchase, then choose Restore purchases in MosaBingo Settings or the Crazy mode hosting-access panel. Valid access on that account can be restored after reinstalling or changing devices. Restoration does not reset the trial. Cancelled purchases or purchases awaiting approval do not immediately grant permanent access.

Why does access verification fail or Start game stay disabled?

Check your internet connection and availability of Apple's purchase services, then choose Retry or Restore purchases. Failed access verification restricts only starting Crazy mode rounds on iOS; other free features remain available. Starting a game also requires at least two players, readiness and completed synchronization. Purchased access does not replace those room requirements.

Purchase or refund questions?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Visit Report a Problem to check purchases or request a refund. Submitting a request does not mean a refund has been approved. If access is missing in the app, restore purchases first, then email us the message shown and app version. We do not need your account password.

Data and privacy

Game boards and settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app may remove local data, and there is no cloud game backup. See the iOS Privacy Policy or Android Privacy Policy.
